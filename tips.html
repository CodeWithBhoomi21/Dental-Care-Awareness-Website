Dental Care Tips

Simple Habits for a Healthy Smile!

1. Brush Twice a Day

Brush your teeth for two minutes in the morning and before sleeping.

2. Eat Healthy Food

Eat fruits, vegetables and calcium-rich foods for healthy teeth.

3. Avoid Too Much Sugar

Reduce sugary drinks, chocolates and sweets to prevent tooth decay.

4. Drink Enough Water

Drink water regularly to keep your mouth hydrated.

5. Visit Your Dentist

Get regular dental checkups to maintain good oral health.
