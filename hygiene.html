What is Oral Hygiene?

Oral hygiene means keeping your teeth, gums and mouth clean and healthy. Good oral hygiene prevents dental problems.

1. Brush Twice Daily

Brush your teeth for two minutes, twice a day, using fluoride toothpaste.

2. Floss Regularly

Use dental floss to clean between your teeth and remove food particles.

3. Clean Your Tongue

Clean your tongue gently to remove bacteria and maintain fresh breath.

4. Visit Your Dentist

Visit a dentist regularly for dental checkups and professional cleaning when needed.
