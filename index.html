Welcome to Dental care

A healthy Smile starts with proper dental care. Learn how to keep your teeth clean and healthy.

Oral Hygiene

Follow these simple habits for healthy teeth.

🪥 Brushing

Brush your teeth twice daily using fluoride toothpaste.

😁 Flossing

Clean between your teeth regularly using dental floss.

👅 Tongue Cleaning

Clean your tongue daily to maintain oral hygiene.

💧 Rinsing

Rinse your mouth with water after meals.

Dental Procedures

Learn about common dental treatments.

🦷 Teeth Cleaning

Removes plaque and tartar to maintain healthy teeth.

✨ Tooth Filling

Repairs teeth damaged by cavities.

😁 Braces

Helps straighten teeth and improve alignment.

🩺 Root Canal

Treats infection inside a damaged tooth.

Dental Care Tips

Follow these tips for a healthy smile.

🪥 Brush Twice

Brush your teeth two times every day.

🍎 Eat Healthy

Eat fruits and vegetables for better oral health.

🚫 Avoid Sugar

Limit sugary foods and drinks to protect your teeth.

👩‍⚕️ Visit Dentist

Visit your dentist regularly for checkups.

Common Dental Problems

🦷 Tooth Decay

Damage to teeth caused by bacteria and acids.

😁 Cavities

Small holes that develop in the teeth.

🩺 Gum Disease

Infection or inflammation of the gums.

❄️ Sensitivity

Pain or discomfort when consuming hot or cold food.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How many times should I brush?

You should brush twice daily.

2. Why is dental hygiene important?

It helps prevent cavities and gum diseases.

3. How often should I visit a dentist?

Regular dental checkups are important.

4. What causes tooth decay?

Bacteria and acids can damage tooth enamel.
